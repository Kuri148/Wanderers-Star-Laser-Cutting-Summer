Click inside the domain to place your first point.

Pick a wallpaper group above -- the drawable domain, symmetry preview, and vertex labels are all rebuilt from that group's Templates/*_Template.scad construction. Switching groups clears the canvas (coordinate systems differ per group).

Draw mode: click to drop points inside the domain. Clicks outside snap to the nearest boundary point. With Snap on, points lock onto the corners and any existing point (a green ring shows the target). With Bézier on, click then drag to curve a segment. Close loop finishes a shape.
Edit points mode: hover a point or Bézier handle (cursor turns to a hand) and drag it to a new spot. Anchors stay in the domain and snap to corners/points; the output updates live.

The output panel is just the drawn polygon() block(s) -- paste them into that group's FundamentalCut() / shapesToCut().
